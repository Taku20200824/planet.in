Rules

Clean server rules

  1. Respect every player. No harassment, hate speech, or targeted drama.
  2. No cheating, exploit abuse, macro abuse, or client modification.
  3. Keep Discord names readable for staff and reports.
  4. Use report channels for proof, clips, screenshots, and Steam names.
  5. No spam, scam links, NSFW content, or advertising without permission.
  6. Staff decisions can be appealed calmly through #ban-appeal.
